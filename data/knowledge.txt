== FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ==

Q: What documents do I need for tax preparation?
A: Generally, W-2s, 1099s, prior year tax returns, receipts for deductions, and any investment statements. Our team will provide a full checklist during your consultation.

Q: How long does tax preparation take?
A: It depends on the complexity. Simple returns may take a few days. Complex business returns may take one to two weeks.

Q: Can you handle both personal and business taxes?
A: Yes. We handle personal, business, and multi-entity tax situations.

Q: Do you work with clients remotely?
A: Yes. We work with clients across the United States. Most services are available virtually via phone or video call.

Q: What if I received an IRS notice?
A: Do not ignore it. Contact us immediately. We provide full IRS audit representation and response services.

Q: Can I get help with back taxes?
A: Yes. We assist clients with unfiled returns, back taxes, and IRS payment plan negotiations. Please let our team know your situation so they can advise accordingly.

Q: Are your services confidential?
A: Absolutely. All client information is handled with strict confidentiality in accordance with professional standards and applicable law.

Q: How do I schedule an appointment?
A: You can schedule through Reba (this receptionist), through our website, or by speaking with our team directly.
